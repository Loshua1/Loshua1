My best project (and by far my favorite so far) is my Digital Audio Workstation app, which I called **MusicMani**. It's an app where users can upload any .wav audio file, modify the file, and export it again. Modifications can take place in the terms of pitch, volume, or trimming by manipulating the short arrays of the .wav. This app also allows for the blending of different files into a singular file, creating entirely new files that can be a mix of files that are individually modified.
[Repository](https://github.com/Loshua1/MusicMani)
